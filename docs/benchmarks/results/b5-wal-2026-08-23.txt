Note on client/worker matching: an earlier attempt at --workers 8
--clients 64 under thread-pool produced a workload artifact, not a
measurement, and is deliberately not reported. ThreadPoolServer submits
each connection's entire session as one long-lived task, so a fixed
8-worker pool only actively services 8 of the 64 connections at a time;
the other 56 sit fully queued (accepted, but never read from) until one
of the active 8 disconnects. That distorts pre-populate timing rather
than measuring WAL cost, so all thread-pool numbers above use
clients == workers.
